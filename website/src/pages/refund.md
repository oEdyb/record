# Refund policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

- **14-day money-back guarantee.** If Klippt Pro is not for you, email
  [support@klippt.app](mailto:support@klippt.app) within 14 days of your first payment, monthly or
  yearly, and we refund it in full. No questions.
- **Renewals.** You can cancel any time from the link in your receipt email. You keep access until
  the end of the paid period. We do not refund partial periods, except where the law requires it.
- **How refunds are paid.** Lemon Squeezy, our merchant of record, returns the money to your
  original payment method. It usually arrives within 5–10 business days.

This guarantee is in addition to your rights as a consumer, including the EU right of withdrawal.
