# Support

Email [support@klippt.app](mailto:support@klippt.app). We usually answer within one working day.

Please include your Mac model, your macOS version, the Klippt version (Klippt → About Klippt), and
what you did right before the problem. A screenshot helps.

## Common fixes

- **Klippt records a black screen or no audio.** Open System Settings → Privacy & Security →
  Screen & System Audio Recording, turn Klippt off and on again, then restart Klippt.
- **No camera or microphone.** Check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and Microphone.
- **Updates do not install.** Move Klippt to the Applications folder and open it from there.

## Bugs and feature requests
