# Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Klippt is made by **Dyberg & Co AB** (org. no. 559577-0917), Sweden. We are the controller for the
personal data described here. Contact: [support@klippt.app](mailto:support@klippt.app).

## Your recordings stay on your Mac

Klippt records and stores your screen, camera, microphone and system audio locally. We never
receive your recordings, projects, transcripts, cursor or keyboard activity. The app contains no
analytics and no automatic crash reporting.

## What the app sends, and to whom

- **Update checks, to updates.dyberg.co.** When Klippt checks for an update, it downloads a small
  file from our update server, hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web request, this exposes your IP
  address and app version to Cloudflare.
- **Fonts, to Google.** The current version loads some interface fonts from Google Fonts, which
  exposes your IP address to Google. We are moving these fonts into the app.

## This website

klippt.app uses no cookies and no analytics. Our host, Cloudflare, processes your IP address to
deliver and protect the site.

## Email

When you email us, we keep the conversation to answer you and for up to 24 months afterwards.

## Legal basis and retention

We process update and website requests on our legitimate interest in delivering a working,
secure product.

## Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, or object to its
processing, by emailing [support@klippt.app](mailto:support@klippt.app). You can also complain to
the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY), [imy.se](https://www.imy.se).
