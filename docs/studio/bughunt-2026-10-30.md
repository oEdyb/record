## Fix first

1. **Video playback crashes the pill and can hang the Studio.** A Chromium Media-thread
   SIGTRAP killed the pill renderer in both labs, and an early Studio run showed a black,
   unresponsive replay. Runs with accelerated video decode disabled, or a fresh profile, were
   clean. Lead: two renderers decoding the take's clips with hardware decode. Intermittent;
   cause not isolated. (flow 2, studio-ui "black replay")
2. **The Studio's buttons stay dead forever once the pill is gone.** Commands go to the pill
   with no acknowledgement, and the spinner only clears on the next snapshot. There's no
   handler for the HUD renderer dying. (flow 2)
3. **Replay audio leaks into the next clip.** Resume while replay plays: about 0.5 s of replay
   audio (−20 dB) at the start of the new clip. Reproduced. (review 4)
4. **On Niri, the Studio can be recorded.** Moving it onto the recorded output during resume
   preparation revokes admission without hiding it. Code-verified only. (review 1)
5. **Stop without pausing first leaves the last clip without a transcript, permanently.** It
   says "Pause to transcribe" after the recording has ended. Reproduced in both labs.
   (review 2, flow 1)
6. **A new take shows and copies the previous take's transcript.** Sections are matched by
   clip index without checking `recordingId`. Reproduced. (review 3)
7. **No webcam in the Studio replay.** `studioTake()` copies clips without the webcam
   originals. Review has the camera. Reproduced. (studio-ui 1)
