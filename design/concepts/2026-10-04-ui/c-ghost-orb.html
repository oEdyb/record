Concept

CGhost orb

The recorder almost disappears so you can focus on talking. One orb, one ring, controls only when you reach for them.

Signature moment

The clip-history ring around the orb. Each kept clip is an arc sized by duration, the live one grows red, Remove snaps the newest one away.

Rules this bends

  • Replaces the wide bar and the lower clip-history row with an orb plus ring (layout change from the product doc).
  • Controls auto-hide: hover or focus reveals them, they fade after 2 seconds idle. This is a product behavior change.
  • Idle setup hides behind hover or the chevron, so less is visible at a glance.
  • The orb itself is Record, then Pause or Continue.
