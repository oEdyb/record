Concept

Direction B: Studio instrument

The HUD feels like hardware you trust: machined graphite, recessed modules, keys that physically press.

Signature moment

Live input meters plus the tape clip history. The bar proves recording is really working: you see yourself being heard and each clip being laid down.

Rules this bends

Adds green to the live mic meter (live audio state, so allowed) and a faint brushed surface texture with bevel highlights, which is more tactile than the flat house style. Everything else is monochrome plus red. System audio meter is neutral. The camera strip shows signal presence, not level, because a camera has no level. The bar is taller than today (second row for the tape) and the pill is about 74px tall to carry the meter and mini tape.
